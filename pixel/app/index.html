Pixel Calculator – free scientific calculator and unit converter

Pixel Calculator is a free online calculator. Use the standard keypad or switch to the scientific keypad for sin, cos, tan, inverse functions, logarithms, powers, square roots, factorials, pi and e in degrees or radians. The built-in unit converter handles length, area, volume, weight, temperature, speed, pressure and power. It also has calculation history, color themes, achievements and daily streaks, works offline, and can be installed on your phone from the browser. Download the Android app (APK). Privacy policy.
