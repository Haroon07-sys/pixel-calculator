Privacy Policy

Pixel Calculator · Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Pixel Calculator does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything the app remembers stays on your own device.

What the app stores on your device

To work the way you expect, the app saves a few things locally on your phone or in your browser: your notes, your appearance choices (light or dark, colour theme, button shape, large mode), your keypad choice, and your achievements and daily streak. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage statistics. We do not use cookies for tracking, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission: vibration, used only for the small tap feedback when you press keys. It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or the internet.

Clipboard

If you tap the result to copy it, or tap Copy on the Support page, the app places that text on your clipboard. It never reads what is on your clipboard.

Internet and third parties

The app works fully offline. It does not send your calculations, notes or settings to us or to anyone else, and it does not contain third-party services. The Android app displays its content using the Android System WebView that is already part of your device.

Support page and donations

The Support page only shows a cryptocurrency wallet address that you may choose to copy. The app does not process payments and does not see or record any donation. Any transfer you make happens outside the app, in your own wallet, and is recorded on a public blockchain by that network, not by us.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
